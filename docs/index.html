VIEW & FUNCTION

Lower visibility makes walls fainter. This also controls back-wall shadows. Fences remain visible.

During a walk test, the camera-facing sides of your current room lower or turn transparent together. Rear walls stay up, and stepping outside restores them. Full height, Lower eligible walls, and Transparent walls override this preview.

ROOM AREAS

Use Room area, then click two opposite floor corners. Areas separate rooms at doorways and define open-plan interiors. Earlier areas take priority where they overlap.
